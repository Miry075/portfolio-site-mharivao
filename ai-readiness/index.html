The problem

Agents fail on context, not on code.

Most teams discover these three gaps only after a bad pull request, or a security review that stops the pilot.

01 · domain

They don't know your business

Acronyms, business rules and past architecture decisions live in people's heads, meeting notes and an outdated wiki.

02 · freshness

Stale context is worse than none

A knowledge base nobody maintains goes stale in weeks. The agent then applies rules you retired, with full confidence.

03 · control

No limits, no audit trail

Agents can read secrets, seeds and customer data, and nobody can say what they accessed. Your security team says no.

What you get

Four deliverables, in three days.

A clear picture of where you stand, what blocks a safe rollout, and exactly what to do next. You own everything; no lock-in.

AI-readiness score

A score out of 100 for up to 5 repositories: agent context, freshness, architecture decisions, domain glossary, sensitive-data exposure and measured quality.

Output: score per repo, with the fixes ranked by impact.

Risk register

Where agents could read secrets, test data or personal data today; where they would work from stale or contradictory rules; what your current setup can't audit.

Output: each risk with severity, evidence and the fix.

Access policy draft

Which knowledge each agent may use in each phase (requirements, design, build, test, release), mapped to your Entra ID groups and work-item states. Denied by default.

Output: a versioned policy file, ready for review by security.

8-week rollout plan

Week by week: pilot repos, glossary, reviewed wiki pages, CI checks, evaluation set and the knowledge gateway, with owners and exit criteria for each step.

Output: the plan plus a 1-hour readout with your CTO and security lead.
